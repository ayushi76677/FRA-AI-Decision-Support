# Current limitations

- The supplied files contain state-level aggregates, not individual claims.
- District boundaries, coordinates, parcel IDs, dates, documents, satellite imagery, and field evidence are absent.
- The API therefore cannot truthfully detect delayed individual claims, land-record mismatches, satellite change, or spatial overlap.
- Pending-rate signals are operational triage only and do not establish illegality, fraud, eligibility, or rejection.
- A real claim-level deployment requires authoritative identifiers, dates, geometry, evidence provenance, role permissions, and database credentials.
