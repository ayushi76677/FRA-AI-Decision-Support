# Research methodology

The supplied research material supports a modular evidence workflow: temporal Sentinel-2/Landsat observations can identify possible land-cover change; GIS can represent change polygons and intersections; field verification can provide contextual ground truth; and community evidence can record confirmation, contestation, or uncertainty. The application must preserve evidence for and limitations together because satellite observations do not establish the cause or legal meaning of change.

When imagery and labels become available, the planned evaluation records precision, recall, F1, IoU, false-positive rate, and false-negative rate for change detection. LULC classifiers such as Random Forest and SVM should store features, training data, version, parameters, and validation metrics. No research-paper metric is treated as an application result.
